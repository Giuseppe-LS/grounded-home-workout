Easy plate guide

½ vegetables/fruit · ¼ protein · ¼ potatoes/rice/pasta/bread/grains, plus a sensible fat source.

This is a flexible visual guide, not a rule for every meal.

Training days

Don't train deliberately under-fuelled. A normal meal 2–3 hours before is fine. If hungry closer to training, choose something simple such as yogurt + fruit or a banana. Afterwards, a normal meal containing protein and carbohydrates is enough.

About body weight

Day-to-day weight can move because of hydration, food, salt and the menstrual cycle. Look at the multi-week trend, not individual readings. Strength and consistency are important progress markers too.
